The Eye Behind The Lens

Founded by Oliyad Diriba, DG PRODUCTION was built on an uncompromising obsession: to bridge international Hollywood-grade aesthetics with deeply resonant storytelling.

With years of specialized experience in cinematography and color science, Oliyad approaches each frame not merely as video footage, but as a carefully lit canvas. Every beam of light, every shadow curve, and every cut is calibrated to evoke undeniable emotional impact.

"Cinematography is the quiet language of emotion. When the lighting, pacing, and color grade harmonize, an audience stops watching a screen and starts living the moment."
